# Weekly Research Digests

Archive each weekly digest as `YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Digest standards

Prioritise meaningful work from arXiv, OpenReview, peer-reviewed venues, and frontier research publications.

For each item include:
- Title, authors, date, venue/status, and source link.
- Research question.
- Core contribution and method.
- Evidence and limitations.
- Why it matters to the lab's research pillars.
- Reading priority.

Separate peer-reviewed work, preprints, announcements, and speculation. Prefer primary sources. Avoid hype and do not present a preprint as peer-reviewed.

End each digest with:
- Three recommended readings.
- Important developments or disagreements.
- One research question or small experiment to investigate.

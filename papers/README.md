# Paper Reading Workspace

## Reading workflow

1. Capture the paper's title, authors, venue, publication date, and canonical URL.
2. Identify the research question and why it matters.
3. Summarise the central claim in your own words.
4. Record the method, assumptions, data, and baselines.
5. Separate results from interpretation.
6. Identify limitations, confounders, and missing comparisons.
7. Write what would change your mind.
8. Record one follow-up experiment or question.

## Paper review template

Do not treat a paper's abstract or author's claims as independent verification. Check the method and evidence.
